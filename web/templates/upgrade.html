Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as one generation?

Each time you click "Generate Listing" for a single product photo, that's one generation. Translations and template saves do not count.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel from your Stripe billing portal at any time. Your access stays active until the end of the current billing period.

What happens when I hit the Starter limit?

You'll see a notification when you're close to your 100 generations. At the limit you can upgrade to Pro or wait for your next billing cycle to reset.

Is my shop data secure?

We only request the minimum permissions needed to read and create listings. We never store your products or images beyond the time needed to generate the listing.
