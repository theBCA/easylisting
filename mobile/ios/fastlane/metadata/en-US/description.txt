EasyListing turns your product photos and a short description into a complete, ready-to-publish e-commerce listing in seconds — titles, descriptions, tags, materials, and even a suggested price, all generated by AI.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick up to 5 product photos from your camera roll
2. Type a short hint about the item (e.g. "handmade ceramic mug, 350 ml")
3. Choose your target language and marketplace
4. Tap Generate — your full listing appears in seconds
5. Review, edit, and publish directly to your shop

KEY FEATURES
• AI-powered listing generation — titles, SEO tags, descriptions, materials
• Publish directly to Etsy — no copy-paste needed
• Trendyol listing support with Turkish price suggestions
• Bulk upload mode for creating many listings at once
• AI listing improver — rewrite titles for SEO, warm up descriptions, shorten text
• AI photo variants — generate lifestyle/studio shots from your product photos
• Multi-language output — English, Turkish, German, and more

DESIGNED FOR SELLERS
Whether you run a small handmade shop or manage hundreds of SKUs, EasyListing saves hours of writing time every week. Connect your Etsy shop with one tap and publish straight from your phone. More marketplace integrations coming soon.
